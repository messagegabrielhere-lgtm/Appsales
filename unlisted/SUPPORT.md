## Common questions

**An opt-out link doesn't work.** Brokers move their opt-out pages often. Look for "Opt out", "Do Not Sell" or "Privacy" in the site's footer. Please email us the broken link so we can update the app.

**Where is my data stored?** Only on your iPhone. See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).

**How do I delete everything?** Open the Home tab, tap the gear icon, then "Delete all my data".

**Does the app send letters for me?** No. It fills them in and opens them in your Mail app. You review and send them yourself.

**Is this legal advice?** No. Unlisted gives general privacy guidance.
