## Description

Your name, address, phone number, relatives and even your kids' details are for sale on dozens of people-search sites. Unlisted walks your whole family through finding them and taking them down, and locks down your identity while you're at it.

SCAN
Ready-made searches for every name, nickname, city, email, phone number and username in your family. Log what you find, and Unlisted tells you the next step.

REMOVE
Step-by-step opt-outs for 27 of the biggest people-search sites, data brokers and search engines, including Spokeo, Whitepages, BeenVerified, TruePeopleSearch, FastPeopleSearch and the Intelius group. Track every request for every family member, and get reminded to re-check, because brokers re-list people.

LETTERS
Ready-to-send letters, filled in for you:
• Data broker deletion requests
• Website and news removal requests
• "Do not share" requests for banks and insurers
• Child credit freeze letters for all three bureaus
• Wayback Machine removal, with the Internet Archive exclusion form filled in for each site
• License plate camera data requests
You review every word and send it from your own Mail app.

PROTECT
A family checklist for the steps that matter most: credit freezes, IRS Identity Protection PINs, SIM-swap protection, 2-step login, kids' credit freezes, school directory opt-outs, a family safe word against voice-cloning scams, and more. Adults, kids and the household each get their own steps.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
• Everything stays on your iPhone, in a file iOS encrypts
• The app never asks for a Social Security number
• Reminders never show names on your lock screen
• Export or delete all your data any time

Unlisted is free. It gives general privacy guidance, not legal advice.

## App Review notes

No sign-in is required. On first launch, enter any first name to reach the main screens. The app has no server and makes no network requests of its own: searches and opt-out pages open in Safari, and letters open in the Mail composer for the user to send. All data is stored on the device.

Contact for review: messagegabrielhere@gmail.com

## App Privacy (the "nutrition label")

Choose **"No, we do not collect data from this app."** That gives the "Data Not Collected" label. This matches the code: there's no analytics SDK, no server and no tracking, and PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy declares nothing collected.

## Export compliance

Uses encryption: **No** (only Apple's built-in file protection, which is exempt). This is already declared in the build (ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO), so App Store Connect shouldn't ask.
