Fuelprint privacy policy

Last updated October 8, 2026

Fuelprint is a food diary and supplement tracker for iPhone and iPad, published by Pioneer I LLC ("we").

What Fuelprint collects

Nothing. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. Fuelprint has no account, no servers, no analytics and no advertising.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter stays in Fuelprint's private storage on your device: your log of food, drinks, supplements, activity and feelings, your supplement and routine checklist, and your optional profile (age, sex, height, weight, activity level, goals, diet, medical history, medications, allergies and notes). It is protected by iOS data protection and included in your device backups if you have them turned on. Fuelprint never connects to the internet.

Sending a prompt to an AI assistant

The Ask AI screen builds a text prompt from your log on your device. Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to send it:

  • Send to AI copies the prompt to your clipboard and asks iOS to open the assistant you pick, such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot or Grok. Where that service supports it, the prompt is included in the web address so it arrives already typed.
  • Copy Prompt places it on your clipboard.
  • Share hands it to the app you choose in the iOS share sheet.

Once you send a prompt, the service you chose handles it under its own privacy policy and terms. Fuelprint is not affiliated with these services. Your profile is only included when "Include my profile" is on in Ask AI. Check what a service does with your data before sending anything sensitive, especially health information.

Importing and Shortcuts

Import reads only the file or text you choose, on your device, and adds what you approve. The Get AI Prompt action in the Shortcuts app returns your prompt to the shortcut you run; where it goes next is decided by that shortcut.

Health information and HIPAA

Fuelprint is a personal tool, not a healthcare provider, health plan or service working for one. We never receive, store or have access to your health information, so it is not held by a HIPAA covered entity or business associate. Because we collect nothing, there is nothing for us to sell, share or disclose.

Not medical advice

Answers from AI assistants can be wrong and are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Talk to a doctor or pharmacist before changing your diet, supplements or medication.

Third parties

Fuelprint contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code. No third party receives any data from Fuelprint.

Deleting your data

Swipe to delete any log entry, clear any profile field, delete a checklist item to remove it and its history, or delete the app to remove everything.

Children

Fuelprint does not collect data from anyone, including children.
