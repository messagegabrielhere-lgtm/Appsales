Dayfile privacy policy

Last updated September 28, 2026

Dayfile is a daily log for food, drinks, activity, supplements and how you feel, for iPhone and iPad.

What Dayfile collects

Nothing. Dayfile does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter, including your log entries, your supplements and routines, and the optional About Me text, is stored in files inside Dayfile's private container on your device. The file is protected by iOS data protection and is included in your device backups if you have backups enabled. Dayfile has no servers and never connects to the internet.

Sharing with AI assistants

Dayfile never contacts an AI service or any other server. The Ask AI screen builds a text summary of your log on your device. Nothing leaves your phone unless you tap Copy for AI or Share to an App. Copy places the text on your clipboard. Share hands it to the app you pick in the standard iOS share sheet. From that point, the app or service you chose handles the text under its own privacy policy, so check it before sharing anything sensitive.

Your About Me text is only included when the Include About Me switch is on.

Third parties

Dayfile contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. No third party receives any data from Dayfile.

Deleting your data

Swipe to delete any log entry, delete a checklist item to remove it and its history, or delete the app to remove everything.

Children

Dayfile does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address and the app's listing will be updated.
