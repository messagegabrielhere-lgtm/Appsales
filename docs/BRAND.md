# Fuelprint brand

## Name

**Fuelprint.** The pattern of what fuels you: food, drinks, supplements and activity, and how
you feel after.

You write your day in plain words. An AI assistant reads the log and finds your fuelprint:
what lifts your energy, what drains it, what you actually take and when.

App Store name: `Fuelprint: Food & Supplements` (29 characters; Apple's limit is 30).
Subtitle: `Log your day. Ask your AI.`
Siri: "Log food in Fuelprint", "Log water in Fuelprint".

### How it was chosen

Sixty candidates were checked against the US App Store with `scripts/check_names.py` (run by
the **Check app names** workflow) in October 2026. About forty already had apps using them.
Fuelprint had none, and it covers the whole app (food, supplements and activity) rather than
only food or only mood.

| Candidate | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Kept | Five or more apps with the same name. Unfindable in search. |
| Hunch | "Hunch Dating App" has 6,000+ ratings. |
| Gut Check, Gutsy, Gutlog | Each taken by IBS and gut trackers, the closest competitors. |
| Spoonful, Eatwell, Plateful | Large food apps with 1,000 to 21,000 ratings. |
| Telltale | No app, but Telltale Games owns the name for software. |
| Sumday | Spoken aloud it is "Sunday". |
| Dayfile | Clear and used briefly, but neutral: it says "file", not food, fuel or feeling. |
| Moodplate, Foodclue | Clear. Strong runners-up; both say food and mood but leave out supplements. |
| **Fuelprint** | **Clear.** No app found under this name. |

Re-check before any major marketing push; names get taken.

## What stays "Kept" internally, on purpose

The bundle ID (`com.messagegabrielhere.kept`), App Group, Xcode module, widget `kind`, and the
Application Support folder holding 1.0 data all keep the old name. Renaming any of them would
orphan existing users' data or remove widgets they already placed. Only what people see
changed, through `Kept/Shared/Brand.swift`.

## Color

A sunrise: warm, food-friendly, and deliberately unlike the sea of green and blue health apps.

| Role | Hex | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gradient start | `#FBBF24` | Amber |
| Gradient middle | `#F97316` | Orange |
| Gradient end | `#E11D48` | Rose |
| Accent, light mode | `#C2410C` | 5.2:1 against white |
| Accent, dark mode | `#EA580C` | 5.9:1 against black; white text on it 3.6:1, fine for bold labels |

Log kinds keep their own hues so they read at a glance: food orange, drink blue, activity green,
feeling pink.

## Icon

A white page with three entry lines cut out of it, and a sparkle clear of its top-right corner,
on the sunrise gradient. The page is the log, the print; the sparkle is the AI; the gradient,
amber to rose, is the fuel. Generated by
`scripts/make_icon.py`, which has no dependencies.

## Voice

Plain, calm, specific. Short sentences. Say what it does, not how it will change your life.

- Yes: "Write it the way you'd say it."
- Yes: "One tap turns your week into a ready prompt."
- No: "Unlock your full wellness potential with AI-powered insights."

Never make health claims. Never suggest a dose. When health comes up, point to a doctor or
pharmacist.
