Common questions

How do streaks work?

A streak counts consecutive days you marked a habit done. Missing a day does not break the streak until the day after, so you always have until midnight to check in.

I forgot to check in yesterday.

Open the habit and tap yesterday on the calendar. Any past day can be toggled.

The widget isn't updating.

Widgets refresh the moment you check in from the app or the widget itself. If one looks stale, open Kept once; iOS occasionally pauses widget refreshes to save battery. Removing and re-adding the widget also resets it.

I'm not getting reminders.

Reminders only fire on scheduled days you haven't checked in yet. If none arrive at all, open Settings, Notifications, Kept, and make sure Allow Notifications is on.

What is a rest day?

When you edit a habit you can choose which weekdays it applies to. The other days are rest days: they never break a streak, and reminders stay quiet.

Does my data sync between devices?

No. Kept stores everything on the device and never connects to the internet. To move your data, use Export in the About screen.

How do I delete my data?

Delete a habit to remove it and its history, or delete the app to remove everything.
