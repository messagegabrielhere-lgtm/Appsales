How to use AI as your food diary

Calorie apps make you search a database for every bite. AI assistants like ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini can instead read a diary written in plain words, like "two eggs, toast and black coffee", and estimate calories, protein, carbs, fat and fiber, stating the portions they assumed.

The method

  1. Write everything down as you go, one line per thing: meals, drinks, supplements, workouts and how you feel.
  2. Add context once: age, height, weight, activity level and goals. Estimates for protein and calories depend on them.
  3. Ask a specific question about a specific period, such as "estimate my protein per day for the last 7 days and show the gaps".
  4. Ask for ranges, not exact numbers, and ask the AI to list its assumptions.

Questions that work well

  • Estimate calories, protein, carbs, fat and fiber per day, with a weekly average.
  • Compare my protein with common ranges for my weight and goal.
  • What seems to lift or drain my energy? Cite the days.
  • Plan tomorrow's meals from foods I already eat.
  • Make a grocery list for next week with a few healthier swaps.
