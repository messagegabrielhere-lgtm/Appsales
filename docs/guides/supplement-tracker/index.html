A supplement and vitamin tracker that works with AI

Most people who take supplements can't say how consistently they took them last month, or whether their timing makes sense. A simple daily checklist fixes the first; an AI review helps with the second.

What to track

  • Each supplement with its dose, such as Vitamin D 2000 IU, Magnesium glycinate 400 mg, Creatine 5 g or Fish oil 1 g.
  • When you take it, and which days it applies to.
  • What you ate and drank the same day, since some supplements are commonly taken with food or apart from coffee.

What to ask an AI

  • How consistently did I take each item this month?
  • Which are commonly taken with food, apart from each other, or at a particular time of day?
  • Which combinations are worth asking my pharmacist about?

Frame every answer as a question for your pharmacist or doctor, not an instruction.
