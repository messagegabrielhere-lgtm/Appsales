## Description (4000 characters max)

```
Fuelprint is an AI nutrition log, food diary and supplement tracker. Log meals, drinks, vitamins, creatine and workouts by typing, speaking or scanning a label, then get calorie, protein and nutrition estimates, patterns and plans from the AI assistant you already use. Pay once. No subscription.

No food database. No weighing. Say "two eggs, toast and black coffee, then five grams of creatine" and you're done. Or paste your whole day, or a whole month, as a list.

SPEAK, TYPE, SCAN OR REPEAT
• Speak your day: talk the way you'd tell a friend and Fuelprint splits it into entries, recognized privately on your iPhone
• Type or paste a list, one thing per line: "5g creatine", "steak salad", "30 min walk"
• Scan a label: photograph a Nutrition Facts or Supplement Facts panel and the calories, protein and doses are read on your iPhone
• Repeat yesterday in one tap for days that look the same
• Add date lines such as 10/06/26, "Monday" or "Yesterday" to log many days at once
• Each line is sorted into food, drink, supplement, activity, feeling or other, and you can change any guess with a tap
• Move your history in from Apple Notes, a CSV export from another tracker, or a Fuelprint backup

SEE YOUR TRENDS
• Paste an AI answer back and its daily calories, protein and fiber are charted
• Water, how you felt, and supplement consistency over 30 days, 90 days or a year

SUPPLEMENT AND VITAMIN TRACKER
• A daily checklist with doses, such as Vitamin D 2000 IU or Creatine 5 g
• Reminders at the time you choose
• Streaks, adherence stats and a twelve-month view

ASK AI: 15 READY-MADE QUESTIONS, ANY PERIOD
• Nutrition estimate: calories, protein, carbs, fat and fiber per day
• Protein intake check against your weight and goals
• Weight goal check, gut health and bloating, sleep and energy, hydration, caffeine and alcohol
• Supplement review, framed as questions for your doctor
• Weekly review, tomorrow's meal plan and a grocery list
• A doctor visit summary to bring to your appointment
• Or ask your own question, over any period from today to all time
• Include Apple Health: steps, workouts, sleep, weight and resting heart rate

SEND IT ANYWHERE
On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, get an answer right in the app, made privately on your iPhone. Or tap Send to AI and pick Grok, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any AI app: your whole prompt arrives in its message box. Long periods go as a file.

MY PROFILE
Add your age, height, weight, activity level, goals, diet, medical history, medications and allergies once. Every prompt starts with them, so answers fit you. Leave anything blank.

WIDGETS, SIRI AND SHORTCUTS
• Check off supplements and add water from your home screen
• "Hey Siri, log food in Fuelprint," then say what you had
• The Get AI Prompt action hands your log to any AI action in the Shortcuts app

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Fuelprint never connects to the internet. No account, no analytics, nothing uploaded. Speech and label photos are read on your iPhone. Your log and profile stay on your phone. When you send a prompt, you choose exactly where it goes. Export everything as a spreadsheet or a full backup at any time.

PAY ONCE
No subscription. No in-app purchases. No ads.

IMPORTANT
AI answers can be wrong and are not medical advice. Fuelprint does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice. Talk to a doctor or pharmacist before changing your diet, supplements or medication.
```

## App Privacy

Unchanged: **Data Not Collected**. The app has no network code. Speech is recognized with
on-device recognition only, label photos are read with on-device Vision text recognition and
not stored, and Apple Health data is read on the device only to put into a prompt the user
chooses to send. None of it reaches the developer.

## App Review notes (4000 characters max)

```
No account, no sign-in, no network access anywhere in the app. Fully functional offline on a fresh install.

NEW IN 2.3 (includes 2.2)
1. Speak: Today > Speak. Asks for microphone and speech recognition permission, then transcribes on the device only (requiresOnDeviceRecognition). Tap the microphone, say "two eggs and toast, then five grams of creatine", tap Done. The entries open in the list editor for review before Add.
2. Scan label: Today > Scan label. Take a Photo (camera permission) or Choose a Photo of a Nutrition Facts or Supplement Facts panel. Text is read on the device with the Vision framework; the photo is not saved. The result opens in the list editor for review.
3. Repeat yesterday: Today > Repeat yesterday opens the previous day's food, drinks and supplements in the list editor to review before Add.
4. Apple Health (HealthKit, read only): Ask AI > step 3 > Include Apple Health. Requests read access to steps, exercise minutes, active energy, sleep, body mass, resting heart rate and workouts. Values are added to the prompt text only, never stored by the app, never written to Health, and never sent anywhere by the app. HealthKit data is not used for advertising or shared with third parties.
5. Trends: History > Trends. Nutrition prompts ask the AI to end with a small FUELPRINT-DAILY block; copying the AI's answer and tapping Paste (Ask AI step 4, or Trends) charts the daily estimates.
6. Answer on This iPhone: in Send to AI on devices with Apple Intelligence (iOS 26+). Uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework; hidden on unsupported devices.
7. Send to AI now leads with "Send to an AI App", the system share sheet, so the prompt goes into the assistant app the user picks. The assistant rows copy the prompt and open the service's public website or app.
8. The standard StoreKit rating prompt may appear after a completed checklist.

HOW TO REACH EVERY FEATURE
1. First launch shows a short welcome with common supplements. Pick two, tap Add and Continue.
2. Today: Type a list, Speak, Scan label and Repeat are in one row. Tap Food, type "Oatmeal with berries", Save. Tap + next to Water.
3. Tap a supplement's circle to check it off; tap its row for streaks and a twelve-month view.
4. History lists every day with data; Trends is at the top.
5. Ask AI: pick a period and a question, then Send to AI. The first time, a notice explains that AI answers are not medical advice; tap I Understand.
6. Settings (gear on Today): glass size, checklist, My Profile, import from Notes or other apps, the AI notice, CSV and JSON export.
7. Widgets: long-press the home screen, Edit, Add Widget, search Fuelprint.
8. Siri: "Log food in Fuelprint". Shortcuts: Get AI Prompt.

HEALTH
Fuelprint records what the user types, says and taps. It makes no health claims and gives no advice or diagnosis. HealthKit is read only, on request, to include in a prompt. Before the first prompt is sent, a notice says AI answers can be wrong, are not medical advice, and that users should talk to a doctor or pharmacist before changing diet, supplements or medication, and call emergency services in an emergency. Every prompt ends by telling the AI it is not the user's doctor. Fuelprint is not affiliated with any AI service it opens.
```
