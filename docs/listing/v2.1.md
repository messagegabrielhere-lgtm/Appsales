# App Store listing copy for version 2.1

Use this ONLY with a 2.1 build: type or paste a list, Supplement and Other entry types, My
Profile, Ask AI with fifteen questions and Send to AI, and the not-medical-advice notice.

> Every claim below must be demonstrable in the binary you attach. Apple rejects under
> Guideline 2.1 App Completeness when the description or screenshots show features the
> build does not have.

Character counts are checked by `scripts/check_listing.py`. `scripts/app_store_connect.py`
reads this file and fills in App Store Connect from it.

## Category

Primary **Health & Fitness**, secondary **Food & Drink**.

## Name (30 characters max)

Unchanged from 2.0, so the app keeps its search history under this name.


The subtitle is the second most heavily weighted text for search after the name. These four

## Description (4000 characters max)

```
Fuelprint is a food diary and supplement tracker built for AI. Write what you eat and drink the way you'd say it, then get calorie, protein and nutrition estimates, patterns and plans from the AI assistant you already use.

No food database. No weighing. No barcode scanning. Type "two eggs, toast and black coffee" and you're done. Or paste your whole day, or a whole month, as a list.

TYPE OR PASTE A LIST
• One thing per line, like a note: "5g creatine", "steak salad", "30 min walk"
• Add a date line such as 10/06/26 to log many days at once
• Fuelprint sorts each line into food, drink, supplement, activity, feeling or other, and you can change any guess with a tap
• Paste the same list again and only new lines are added

LOG IN SECONDS
• Big buttons for food, drinks, activity and how you feel
• Your recent entries come back as one-tap suggestions
• One tap adds a glass of water
• Rate how you feel, and note sleep, mood, energy or digestion

SUPPLEMENT AND VITAMIN TRACKER
• A daily checklist with doses, such as Vitamin D 2000 IU or Creatine 5 g
• Reminders at the time you choose
• Streaks, adherence stats and a twelve-month view
• Lines in a pasted list that name a supplement tick it off

ASK AI: 15 READY-MADE QUESTIONS
• Nutrition estimate: calories, protein, carbs, fat and fiber per day
• Protein check against your weight and goals
• Weight goal check: your energy needs against what you eat
• Gut health, sleep and energy, hydration, caffeine and alcohol
• Supplement review and heart-healthy habits, framed as questions for your doctor
• Weekly review, tomorrow's meal plan and a grocery list
• A doctor visit summary to bring to your appointment
• Or ask your own question

Pick a period, pick a question, tap Send to AI. Fuelprint opens popular assistants like ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini with your prompt ready, or copy and share it anywhere.

MY PROFILE
Add your age, height, weight, activity level, goals, diet, medical history, medications and allergies once. Every prompt starts with them, so answers fit you. Leave anything blank.

WIDGETS AND SIRI
• Check off supplements and add water from your home screen
• "Hey Siri, log food in Fuelprint," then say what you had

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Fuelprint never connects to the internet. No account, no analytics, nothing uploaded. Your log and profile stay on your phone. When you send a prompt, you choose exactly where it goes. Export everything as a spreadsheet or a full backup at any time.

PAY ONCE
No subscription. No in-app purchases. No ads.

IMPORTANT
AI answers can be wrong and are not medical advice. Fuelprint does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice. Talk to a doctor or pharmacist before changing your diet, supplements or medication.
```

## Keywords (100 characters max, comma-separated, no spaces after commas)

Words already in the name and subtitle (fuelprint, food, supplements, ai, calorie, protein,
diary) are indexed from there, so they are not repeated. Apple combines words across the
name, subtitle and keywords, so "calorie" + "counter" matches "calorie counter" and "protein"
+ "tracker" matches "protein tracker". Other apps' names are left out on purpose; Guideline
2.3.7 forbids them in keywords.

```
journal,tracker,macro,meal,log,vitamin,creatine,water,nutrition,diet,counter,weight,gut,mood,health
```

## App Privacy

Unchanged: **Data Not Collected**. The app has no network code. The profile, like the log,
stays on the device. Opening an AI assistant's website with a prompt is the user sending their
own text to a service they chose, not collection by the developer.

## App Review notes (4000 characters max)

```
No account, no sign-in, no network access anywhere in the app. Fully functional offline on a fresh install.

WHAT'S NEW IN 2.1
1. Type or paste a list: on the Today tab, tap "Type or paste a list". Type a few lines such as "Oatmeal", "5g creatine", "30 min walk", or paste several days with date lines like 10/06/26. Each line's type is shown and can be changed by tapping its icon. Tap Add.
2. Two new entry types, Supplement and Other, in the entry editor.
3. My Profile: Ask AI > step 3 > My profile (also in Settings). Optional age, height, weight, goals, diet, medical history, medications and allergies. Stored only on the device and only included in a prompt the user chooses to send.
4. Ask AI: fifteen question templates in five groups. The orange Ask AI card on Today opens the same screen.
5. Send to AI: the button at the bottom of Ask AI. The first time, a notice explains that AI answers are not medical advice; tap I Understand. The send sheet lists ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot and Grok. Tapping one copies the prompt to the clipboard and opens that service's public website (or its app, if installed) with the prompt in the address where the site supports it. The app itself makes no network request; iOS opens the link. Copy Prompt and Share are also there. The app is not affiliated with these services.

HOW TO REACH EVERY FEATURE
1. First launch shows a short welcome with common supplements. Pick two, tap Add and Continue.
2. Today: tap Food, type "Oatmeal with berries", Save. Tap + next to Water. Tap Feeling, choose a face, Save.
3. Tap a supplement's circle to check it off; tap its row for streaks and a twelve-month view.
4. Use the arrows at the top right to step back a day.
5. History lists every day with data.
6. Ask AI: pick a period and a question, then Send to AI.
7. Settings (gear on Today): glass size, checklist, My Profile, the AI notice, CSV and JSON export.
8. Widgets: long-press the home screen, Edit, Add Widget, search Fuelprint.
9. Siri: "Log food in Fuelprint", then say what you ate.

HEALTH
Fuelprint records what the user types and taps. It makes no health claims, gives no advice or diagnosis, and does not use HealthKit. Before the first prompt is sent, a notice says AI answers can be wrong, are not medical advice, and that users should talk to a doctor or pharmacist before changing diet, supplements or medication, and call emergency services in an emergency. The same notice is on the Ask AI screen and in Settings, and every prompt ends by telling the AI it is not the user's doctor.
```
