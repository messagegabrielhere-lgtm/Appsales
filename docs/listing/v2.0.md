## Description (4000 characters max)

```
Kept is a habit tracker that stays out of your way.

Add the small things you want to do every day. Tap the circle when you've done them. Watch the streak grow. That's it.

WHY KEPT

• Pay once. $0.99, every feature, forever. No subscription, no "pro" tier, no upsell inside the app.
• Widgets that work. Check off habits right on your home screen without opening the app. Lock-screen widgets show today's progress at a glance. Widgets update the instant you check in.
• Rest days, not broken streaks. Set a habit for weekdays only, or any days you choose. Days you skip never break a streak.
• Reminders that know when to stay quiet. A daily nudge at the time you pick, skipped automatically once you've checked in and on rest days.
• Your whole year at a glance. A twelve-month heatmap on every habit, alongside current streak, best streak, total days, and your last-30-day rate.
• Forgiving streaks. A streak isn't broken until the next scheduled day, so you're never shown a zero before you've had the chance to check in.
• Fix the past. Forgot to tap yesterday? Tap the day on the calendar.
• Siri and Shortcuts. "Mark drink water done in Kept."
• Private by design. Kept never connects to the internet. There is no account and nothing is uploaded, ever. Your data is one file on your device, protected by iOS encryption, and you can export it as plain JSON in one tap.
• Made for iPhone and iPad, light and dark mode, Dynamic Type, and VoiceOver.

WHAT KEPT DOESN'T DO

Kept doesn't sync between devices, doesn't track you, and doesn't ask you to rate it. It does one thing well and lets you get on with your day.
```

## App Review notes

```
Local-only habit tracker. No account, no sign-in, no network access anywhere in the app.

How to exercise every feature:
1. On first launch, tap two or three suggested habits, then "Add habits".
2. Tap the circle on a row to mark a habit done for today. Tap again to undo.
3. Tap a row to open it: current streak, best streak, total days, 30-day rate, a
   twelve-month heatmap, and a month calendar. Tap any past day to fix a missed check-in.
4. Tap the ... menu, then Edit, to set which weekdays the habit applies to (skipped days
   are rest days and never break a streak) and to turn on a daily reminder.
5. Widgets: long-press the home screen, add the Kept widget in small, medium, or large.
   The check circles in the medium and large widgets work without opening the app.
   Lock-screen widgets are available under Customize on the lock screen.
6. Siri: "Mark drink water done in Kept."
```
