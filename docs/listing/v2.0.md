## Description (4000 characters max)

```
Kept is a habit tracker that stays out of your way.

Add the small things you want to do every day. Tap the circle when you've done them. Watch the streak grow. That's it.

WHY KEPT

• Pay once. $0.99, every feature, forever. No subscription, no "pro" tier, no upsell inside the app.
• Widgets that work. Check off habits right on your home screen without opening the app. Lock-screen widgets show today's progress at a glance. Widgets update the instant you check in.
• Rest days, not broken streaks. Set a habit for weekdays only, or any days you choose. Days you skip never break a streak.
• Reminders that know when to stay quiet. A daily nudge at the time you pick, skipped automatically once you've checked in and on rest days.
• Your whole year at a glance. A twelve-month heatmap on every habit, alongside current streak, best streak, total days, and your last-30-day rate.
• Forgiving streaks. A streak isn't broken until the next scheduled day, so you're never shown a zero before you've had the chance to check in.
• Fix the past. Forgot to tap yesterday? Tap the day on the calendar.
• Siri and Shortcuts. "Mark drink water done in Kept."
• Private by design. Kept never connects to the internet. There is no account and nothing is uploaded, ever. Your data is one file on your device, protected by iOS encryption, and you can export it as plain JSON in one tap.
• Made for iPhone and iPad, light and dark mode, Dynamic Type, and VoiceOver.

WHAT KEPT DOESN'T DO

Kept doesn't sync between devices, doesn't track you, and doesn't ask you to rate it. It does one thing well and lets you get on with your day.
```

## App Review notes

Apple asked for this in the Notes field after the new-account information request.
Full answers and the screen-recording steps are in `docs/REVIEW_REPLY.md`.

```
No account, no sign-in, no credentials needed. Kept works fully offline on a fresh install.

PURPOSE
A habit tracker for everyday personal routines such as drinking water, walking, or reading.
One-time $0.99 purchase, no subscription, no account, no ads, no analytics. General consumer
audience. Not a medical, health, clinical, fitness or enterprise product; it makes no health
claims and records only whether the user tapped a button on a calendar day.

HOW TO REACH EVERY FEATURE
1. Launch. Tap + (top right), type a name, pick an icon and color, tap Save.
2. Tap the circle on a row to mark it done today; tap again to undo.
3. Tap the row to open it: current streak, best streak, total days, 30-day rate, month calendar.
4. Tap any past day in that calendar to fix a missed check-in.
5. Tap ... (top right) to edit or delete the habit.
4b. Tap i on the main list for About, including one-tap JSON export via the share sheet.

5. Widgets: long-press the home screen, Edit, Add Widget, search Kept, add the medium size.
   The check circles inside the medium and large widgets work without opening the app.
   Lock-screen widgets are under Customize on the lock screen.
6. Siri: "Mark drink water done in Kept."
7. Edit a habit to set which weekdays it applies to (skipped days are rest days and never
   break a streak) and to turn on a daily reminder.

EXTERNAL SERVICES: NONE
The app contains no networking code. No backend or API, no data provider, no authentication,
no payment processor (the App Store handles the purchase), no analytics, attribution, crash
reporting or advertising SDK, no AI or ML service, and no third-party libraries at all. It
links only Apple system frameworks. All data is one JSON file in the app's private container
and is never transmitted. The privacy manifest declares no tracking and no collected data.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES: NONE
Identical in every region and storefront. No geo-gating, no region-specific content, no remote
configuration. The only locale-sensitive behavior is standard iOS date formatting and the first
day of the week in the calendar.

REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL: NONE
No health, medical, finance, gambling, dating or messaging functionality. No HealthKit. No
user-generated content, no social or sharing-between-users features. All artwork is original;
the only other imagery is system-supplied Unicode emoji and SF Symbols.
```
