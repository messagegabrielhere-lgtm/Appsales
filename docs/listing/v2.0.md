Information in App Store Connect when you create the 2.0 version. The bundle ID stays the
same, so existing owners get 2.0 as a normal update and keep their data.

Use this ONLY with a 2.0 build: food, drink, activity and feeling log, supplements and
routines checklist, Ask AI export, widgets, and Siri. With a 1.0 build use `v1.0.md`.

## Description (4000 characters max)

```
Dayfile is the fastest way to log your day, and the easiest way to understand it.

Write what you ate the way you'd say it: "two eggs, toast and a flat white." No searching a food database, no weighing, no barcode scanning. Log drinks, activity, supplements and how you feel the same way. Then tap once and Dayfile turns your day, week or month into a clear, ready-made prompt you can paste into any AI assistant. The AI does the maths: calories, protein, patterns, what seems to lift or drain your energy.

LOG IN SECONDS
• Four big buttons: Food, Drink, Activity, Feeling
• Your recent entries appear as one-tap suggestions, because most of us eat the same breakfast
• One tap adds a glass of water
• Rate how you feel from awful to great, and note sleep, mood, energy or digestion
• Fill in yesterday by stepping back a day

SUPPLEMENTS AND ROUTINES
• A daily checklist with doses, such as Vitamin D 2000 IU or Creatine 5 g
• Reminders at the time you choose, skipped automatically once you've checked in
• Set which days each one applies to. Skipped days never break a streak
• Streaks, adherence stats and a twelve-month view for each item

ASK YOUR AI
Choose a period and what you want to know:
• Energy and mood patterns, with small experiments to test them
• Nutrition estimate: calories, protein, carbs, fat and fibre per day
• Supplement review: consistency, timing, and questions worth asking your pharmacist
• Hydration and caffeine
• Activity and recovery
• Or ask your own question

Add a short About Me once, such as your age, goals or diet, and every prompt includes it. Copy, or share straight to the assistant you already use.

WIDGETS AND SIRI
• Check off supplements and add water from your home screen without opening the app
• Jump straight to logging food, drinks, activity or a feeling from the large widget
• Lock-screen widgets show today's progress
• "Hey Siri, log food in Dayfile," then say what you had

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Dayfile never connects to the internet. There is no account, no analytics, and nothing is uploaded. Your log lives on your phone. When you share a prompt, you choose exactly where it goes. Export everything as a spreadsheet or a full backup at any time.

PAY ONCE
No subscription. No in-app purchases. No ads. Every feature, for as long as you own it.

Dayfile does not give medical advice, and AI answers can be wrong. Talk to a doctor or pharmacist before changing supplements, medication or diet.
```

## App Review notes (4000 characters max)

```
No account, no sign-in, no network access anywhere in the app. Fully functional offline on a fresh install.

WHAT CHANGED IN 2.0
The app is renamed from Kept to Dayfile; the bundle ID is unchanged. It is now a daily log for food, drinks, activity and how the user feels, alongside a checklist of supplements and routines. The Ask AI tab turns a period of the log into a text prompt the user can copy or share.

HOW TO REACH EVERY FEATURE
1. First launch shows a short welcome with common supplements. Pick two, tap Add and Continue.
2. Today tab: tap Food, type "Oatmeal with berries", tap Save. It appears in the Log section.
3. Tap + next to Water to add a glass. Tap Feeling, choose a face, Save.
4. Tap a supplement's circle to check it off. Tap its row to see streaks, stats and a twelve-month view.
5. Use the arrows at the top right to step back to yesterday and log for that day.
6. History tab lists every day with data. Tap one to view or edit it.
7. Ask AI tab: pick 7 days and a question type, then tap Copy for AI. The text goes to the clipboard. Share to an App opens the standard share sheet.
8. Settings (gear on Today): glass size, checklist management, About Me, CSV and JSON export.
9. Widgets: long-press the home screen, Edit, Add Widget, search Dayfile. The water button and check circles work without opening the app. The large widget's four buttons open the matching editor.
10. Siri: "Log food in Dayfile", then say what you ate. "Log water in Dayfile."

AI AND EXTERNAL SERVICES
The app does not call any AI or other service. Ask AI only builds text locally. The user decides whether to copy it or send it to another app through the iOS share sheet. No API keys, no servers, no third-party SDKs.

HEALTH
Dayfile records what the user types and taps. It makes no health claims, gives no advice or diagnosis, and does not use HealthKit. The prompt templates tell the AI to state that its answers are not medical advice, and the supplement template tells it not to recommend starting, stopping or changing anything. The Ask AI screen shows the same disclaimer.
```
