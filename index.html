Questions

Can I paste my food diary from the Notes app?

Yes. Paste one thing per line. Date lines such as 10/06/26 split it into days, so a whole month comes in at once, and pasting the same list again only adds new lines.

Does Fuelprint count calories?

Fuelprint has no food database. You write meals in plain words and the AI assistant you choose estimates calories, protein, carbs, fat and fiber, stating the portions it assumed.

Which AI assistants does it work with?

Any. Send to AI opens ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot or Grok with your prompt ready, and you can copy or share it to any other app.

Is my data private?

Yes. Fuelprint has no account and never connects to the internet. Your log and profile stay on your phone until you choose to send a prompt.

Is it medical advice?

No. AI answers can be wrong and are not medical advice. Talk to a doctor or pharmacist before changing your diet, supplements or medication.
